# L8 Show a success message with Jinja

## What we gained

- The route passes `msg_sent=True` to `render_template`, and `contact.html` chooses what to show.
- Jinja `{% if msg_sent %}` shows the heading *Successfully sent your message*, otherwise the normal heading and the form.
- The same template therefore serves the form, the success state and the error state.

## Where it is in the code

- `src/html_forms/presentation/features/blog/templates/contact.html`
- `src/html_forms/constants.py` (`MSG_SENT_TEXT`)
